About Me

A story still
being written.

I'm currently a freshman planning to pursue a career in healthcare. My life wasn't always — and still isn't fully — planned out, but I've been drawn to helping others for as long as I can remember.

Even as a kid, I wanted to help heal.

I was so intrigued by Rapunzel's magical healing hair that I may have attempted to use my own hair to cure a few papercuts over the years.

👥

Whether it's lending a hand at the library or taking responsibility for my own younger brother, I find meaning in caring for people and helping them feel seen and supported.

📖

I'm still exploring healthcare, wondering if I want to go into pediatrics, psychology, or something else — but I'm excited to be learning at every pothole and fork in the road.

✨

I'm excited to continue growing, discovering new interests, building relationships, and finding meaningful ways to make a difference.

“I'm not trying to have every answer figured out. I'm excited to discover where the road takes me.”
